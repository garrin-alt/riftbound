# The Vendetta — Scoring Guide (shareable assets)

Player-facing explainer for the Gulf Nexus League Riftbound rivalry scoring:
one ladder, **The Reckoning**, ranked on **Reckoning Points (RP)**.

Download and share these however you like — WhatsApp, print, or a web page.

## The scoring, in full

| Result | Points |
|---|---|
| Win a match | **3 RP** |
| Draw a match | **1 RP** |
| Lose a match | 0 |
| Beat your declared Vendetta | **+2 RP** on top of the win (5 total) |

Every match scores on its own, so your total depends only on your own results.
Byes never score. A rival who drops just means the bonus never fires, and players
who arrive without a declared Vendetta still earn full match points — they simply
have no bonus to claim.

## Point names

- **Reckoning Points (RP)** — the league's one currency, earned from your own matches.

These display names are also published in the league gist under `rivalry.display`,
so a public standings site can read the board and point labels straight from the data.
