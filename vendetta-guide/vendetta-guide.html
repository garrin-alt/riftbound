The Vendetta

⚔

Every match you've ever played is on the record. Each event it names your enemy — and then keeps score of the grudge, win or lose.

⚔
Who you fight

You don't pick your rival. The record does.

Before each event the system reads your full match history and assigns you a Vendetta — the fiercest matchup it can find among the players in the room. It arrives in one of three grades.

🔥

Sworn Rivals

You've clashed again and again. The history runs deep and the score is never settled.

🩸

Bad Blood

Some history between you — the best grudge available among today's field.

🎲

New Blood

No history yet. A fresh rivalry, drawn at random, waiting to be written.

⚔
How you score

Every match you play is worth points.

One currency — League Points (LP) — and three lines of rules. That really is all of it.

Win a match
3LP
Draw a match
1LP
Lose a match
0None

Nothing else adds to your total — not the draw you were given, not who you were paired against, and not beating your Vendetta. Your score is yours alone.

⚔
The fine print

Three things worth knowing.

⚔ The grudge is worth nothing.

Beating your Vendetta pays exactly what any other win pays — 3 LP. What it earns you is the record: the head-to-head, the highlight, and the bragging rights until you meet again.

◇ No rival today? Nothing changes.

Odd field, rival dropped, or you rolled in late — your score is untouched either way. The Vendetta decides the story, never the standings.

✕ Byes never score.

A bye isn't a fight. It earns no points and doesn't touch your record — for you or your rival. Only battles at the table move the board.
