Every match you've ever played is on the record. Each event it names your enemy — and hands you two ways to make them pay.

⚔
Who you fight

You don't pick your rival. The record does.

Before each event the system reads your full match history and assigns you a Vendetta — the fiercest matchup it can find among the players in the room. It arrives in one of three grades.

🔥

Sworn Rivals

You've clashed again and again. The history runs deep and the score is never settled.

🩸

Bad Blood

Some history between you — the best grudge available among today's field.

🎲

New Blood

No history yet. A fresh rivalry, drawn at random, waiting to be written.

⚔

⚔
Two things worth knowing

🏟 No rival in the room? Duel the field.

If the field is odd and you're left without a Vendetta — or you rolled in late — a clean round win still earns you +2 Shadow. Show up early and prepared and you're given a real rival; latecomers fight the field.

✋ Byes never score.

A bye isn't a fight. It earns no Reckoning Points (RP) and no Shadow Points (SP) — for you or your rival. Only battles at the table move the boards.

⚔
Where it all lands

Two boards. Two crowns.

Your two kinds of points are ranked entirely separately — a rival who never meets you at the table can still be hunting you in the Shadow.

⚔

The Reckoning

Points won face to face, against the Vendetta you were handed. The record of grudges settled in the open.

🌙

The Shadows

The proxy war — every round you outlasted a rival you never got to face. Tracked and crowned on its own.

Chase both. The best rivals top them together.
