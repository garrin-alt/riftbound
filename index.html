🏆 Record Results & Award League Points

Upload a round's results CSV. Every match scores on its own — win 3, draw 1, loss 0. Upload multiple rounds from the same event one at a time (e.g. Swiss round 3, then the Top 8 bracket); points stack each time.
Your score depends only on your own results, never on who you were paired against. Vendettas are worth no points — grudge matches are still recorded for the stats and the story. Byes never earn points.

🌙 Post-Event Processing

Couldn't be at the event? Process the whole thing afterwards. Upload every round's results CSV at once (Swiss rounds and Top Cut together — tag each file below). Scoring uses the Vendetta list declared before the event; if none was declared, one is generated retroactively from attendance — including random New Blood pairs — so nobody misses out on points. Each round scores exactly like the live flow, and every match is merged into season head-to-head.
